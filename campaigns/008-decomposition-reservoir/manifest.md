## Mathematical recipes

### D01 — Fourier decomposition
For the 72 observed normalized temperatures, compute real orthonormal DFT DC and cosine/sine coefficients of frequencies j=1..k (k=3 or 6; basis indexed oldest to newest). DC uses 1/sqrt(72), other components sqrt(2/72). Append most recent normalized temperature and unchanged eight calendar/weather features. Fit direct ridge to all 24 targets. This is a low-frequency Fourier projection readout, distinct from C005's calendar harmonic AR regression. Test constant and known sinusoid coefficients, orthogonality and no future influence.

### D02 — causal Haar wavelet projection
Use only the most recent 64 of 72 observed temperatures. Apply orthonormal dyadic Haar transform using pairwise (a+b)/sqrt(2), (a−b)/sqrt(2), recursively decompose approximations down to one. Order coefficients as final approximation, then detail blocks coarse-to-fine, chronologically inside blocks. Keep first m=16 or 32 coefficients, append last temperature and eight unchanged covariates, direct ridge. No symmetric padding or future/centered filters. This is a within-window multiresolution wavelet readout, not a full-series wavelet forecast. Test energy preservation, reconstruction and known step signal.

### D03 — SSA
Use normalized temperature window only, L=24/36, rank r=4/8. Form all lag-L chronological vectors from selected training windows; estimate the uncentered trajectory Gram / vector count and top r orthonormal eigenvectors U in descending eigenvalue order (reject nonidentifiable/rank-deficient requested subspace). SSA recurrent vector R=(sum_j pi_j U[0:L−1,j])/(1−sum_j pi_j²), pi_j=U[L−1,j]. Require denominator >1e−8. At forecast origin iterate recurrence on last L−1 observed then predicted values, 24 steps. Pooled train-window SSA basis, not refit using future evaluation series; no claim of standard per-series SSA with reconstruction. Test low-rank sinusoid recurrence, eigen residual/orthogonality, singular denominator and chronological update.

### D04 — delay DMD
For L=12/24, use consecutive overlapping lag-L states s_t within observed 72-hour training windows only; pairs (s_t,s_{t+1}) remain inside a window. Form G=sum s_t s_t^T and C=sum s_{t+1}s_t^T. Leading eigenbasis U and positive eigenvalues of G yield truncated DMD reduced transition A=U^T C U diag(1/eigenvalue). Start z=U^T s_origin, iterate z←Az and read last coordinate of U z, 24 steps. No future observations, no stability clipping, no claim of physical system identification. Report eigensolver residuals, effective rank, reduced operator norm and rollout magnitude. Test rank-one exponential and a known oscillatory system.

### D05 — EDMD
Use chronological lag-L temperature state, L=6/12; explicit dictionary psi(s)=[1, all s_i, all s_i*s_j for i<=j] in lexicographic order. Fit observable transition K by centered mean-loss ridge for nonconstant coordinates using paired lifted consecutive states inside each selected training window, keep constant observable exactly one (intercept unpenalized). Roll the learned finite-dimensional linear operator forward in lifted space without re-lifting or future covariates; output last temperature's linear coordinate. Report dictionary dimension, transition residual, operator norm and max rollout magnitude. This is a finite EDMD approximation, not proof of a closed Koopman-invariant space. Test identity and affine dynamics, constant preservation, dictionary indexing and recursive prediction.

### D06 — echo-state reservoir
Seeded fixed recurrent ring reservoir of m=64/128 units; each unit receives previous neighbor state with independently seeded sign times .8 (signed cyclic permutation, spectral radius .8), plus independent input weight and bias sampled uniform [−1,1]. At each observed normalized temperature u_t, h_i←(1−leak)h_i+leak*tanh(.8*sign_i*h_{i−1}+w_i*u_t+b_i), simultaneous update, wrap index. Reset h=0 for every 72-hour window, no cross-origin carried state; final h plus last temperature and eight calendar/weather values feed direct ridge lambda .01. No trained recurrent weights; fixed readout is trained. Seeded SplitMix64 with deterministic draw order (sign, weight, bias by unit) and conversion to uniform via high 53 bits. Report actual reservoir size, contraction bound, seed, state norms and readout residual. Test reset invariance, reproducibility, spectral/norm bound, simultaneous update and readout recovery.

### D07 — extreme learning machine
One fixed random tanh hidden layer m=64/128 over all 80 normalized features: w_ij~Uniform[−sqrt(3/80),sqrt(3/80)], bias uniform [−1,1], SplitMix64 high-53-bit uniform, draw each unit's 80 weights then bias in order. No hidden-weight optimization. Fit direct ridge lambda .001/.01 on hidden activations, shared 24 output columns and unpenalized intercept. Report seed, activation variance and solve residual. Test reproducibility, hidden-feature formula, readout fit and no mutation after training.

### D08 — Markov chain
Full eligible training-origin rows; state defined by quantile bins of normalized current temperature (b=8/16, thresholds from sorted training current temperatures at floor(j*N/b), lower bin if equal). Store train-only mean normalized temperature per nonempty bin; require all bins populated or explicitly block. Estimate adjacent-origin transitions only if timestamps differ by exactly 60 minutes, both training; never join a gap. Pooled transition counts with 1e−3 per-cell pseudocount. Hour-conditioned variant uses four UTC six-hour departure-time buckets (00–05,06–11,12–17,18–23) and shrinkage of one pooled row probability mass per state/bucket. At each future lead propagate state probability vector by corresponding pooled or known-hour transition matrix and output expected bin mean; initial one-hot from last observed temperature. Distinct discrete observable Markov chain from C005's hidden switching AR. Test stochastic row sums, probability propagation, UTC wrap and transition gap rejection; report counts, empty buckets and row normalization errors.

### D09 — bounded symbolic regression
Training-only deterministic search over a finite expression grammar using 4096 selected rows. Terminal list in fixed order: last temperature, lags 1/3/6/12/24/48/71 hours, mean of last 6/24/72 hours, last-minus-lag24, then all eight calendar/weather coordinates. Lag zero means index71. Include terminals and unary square, tanh, abs of terminals; binary +,−,× of ordered terminal pairs (allow only i<=j for commutative operators). Depth1 is this one-operator grammar; depth2 additionally applies square/tanh/abs to depth1 nonterminal expressions, and combines the first 32 depth1 nonterminal expressions with the first eight terminals using +,−,×. Freeze exact enumeration order (terminals; unary op then index; binary op then left/right index; depth2 unary before binary). Deduplicate by canonical expression string; retain all depth1 candidates, and first 1024 additional depth2 candidates in enumeration order. No division/log singularities, no random mutations. Standardize candidate features from training only; reject zero/nonfinite variance, no evaluation filtering. Choose at most 4 or 8 terms by greedy forward selection: for each unused candidate compute sum across leads of squared centered residual correlation with the candidate residualized against selected feature span (normalized by its residual sum of squares); pick maximal reduction with enumeration-order ties, stop if no positive reduction >1e−10 in mean normalized squared loss. Refit selected structure by centered ridge lambda .001 after each selection and update residuals; exact scores and selected expressions persisted. All leads share symbolic basis with separate fitted linear coefficients. This is bounded symbolic basis regression (expression-structure search), not an unconstrained genetic programming engine; report candidate count, selected expressions, 24 coefficients and objective trajectory. Test enumerator determinism/depth and candidate cap, synthetic known expression recovery, no leakage and tie rules.
