## Recipes

- F01: joint 80→64→24 dense or N-HiTS64, normalized temperature target, MSE. Controls.
- F02: 24 independently fitted dense 80→h→1 heads, h=8 or 16, each sees original x and only its own lead target; train all heads on same rows and epochs.
- F03: shared dense 82→h→1 (h=16/32), inputs [x, previous normalized temperature, lead/24]. Teacher-forced training on all 24 leads, previous actual training target for j>1, z_0 for j=1. Inference feeds previous predicted value, fixed observed x; no future actual. One scalar optimizer update per lead. Record train/inference discrepancy.
- F04: 24 distinct dense 81→h→1 heads, h=8/16, input [x, previous normalized temperature]. Same teacher-forced training and free-running inference convention as F03, one separate optimizer per lead. This is a one-lag DirRec variant, not full previous-prediction-vector DirRec.
- F05: predict z_j-d_j using joint MLP64 or N-HiTS64, reconstruct z=d+residual. Daily baseline uses observed 24-hour history only.
- F06: predict normalized first differences z_j-z_(j-1) using MLP64/N-HiTS64, cumulatively reconstruct from z_0. MSE on differences; score reconstructed trajectory, not differences.
- F07: learned two-stage correction. Chronologically split selected training rows into first 6144 and last 2048; discard earlier base-fit rows whose 24-hour target window reaches the first correction origin (record count). Base MLP64 trained 12 epochs on earlier rows only; freeze, predict later rows; train separate affine 104→24 or dense 104→16→24 correction on [x, frozen base normalized 24 predictions], residual target z-base. 12 epochs each stage, no refit on correction/evaluation rows. Report both actual counts; smaller base budget is intentional, not equal-compute.
- F08: joint MLP64/N-HiTS64 MAE, subgradient zero at equality.
- F09: joint MLP64/N-HiTS64 Huber with normalized delta=1°C/sd, loss 0.5 e² inside, delta*(abs(e)-0.5delta) outside.
- F10: joint MLP64 pinball tau=.5 (half absolute error; equivalent minimizer to MAE, implementation control), or 80→64→72 shared output for tau=.1/.5/.9, average pinball over 72 outputs, score median head only; track quantile crossings, no rearrangement, no calibrated interval claim (C014 reserved).
- F11: joint MLP64 weighted MSE, early weights 25-j or late weights j, normalized by sum 300, j=1..24.
- F12: joint MLP64 25/26 outputs: 24 normalized temperatures plus mean of normalized 24 targets, optionally end change z_24-z_0. Loss = primary 24-output mean MSE + 0.25*mean squared auxiliary error. Score only primary heads.
- F13: identical F01 MLP64 training with inference clamp [-60,60]°C (broad predeclared plausibility bounds, not asserted station extremes); separate train penalty variant primary MSE plus mean squared distance of prediction outside same bounds in normalized units, no inference clamp. Report fraction outside and number clipped. Bounds are fixed, not estimated from evaluation.
- F14: joint MLP64 primary MSE + 0.25*mean squared predicted-minus-true first-difference error (23 adjacent future pairs), or second-difference error (22 triples). Both compare trajectory derivatives to observed training trajectory, not arbitrary smoothing. Report raw temperature MAE as primary and derivative diagnostics separately.
