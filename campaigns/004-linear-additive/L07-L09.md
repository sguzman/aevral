# L07–L09 implementation specification
Use identical C003 normalized features; center X and Y by train-only means. Reuse centered Gram G=X'X/n and cross-covariance S=X'Y/n. PCR chooses leading eigenspace of G and solves projected least squares. PLS2 chooses leading left singular vector of residual S from eigensystem S'S, then score variance, X and Y loadings; deflate X/Y via covariance identities and maintain original-coordinate weight transformation. RRR solves full-rank OLS spectrally, then projects fitted responses onto leading eigenvectors of S'B_OLS (symmetrized); not raw target PCA. All twelve declared ranks unchanged. Cyclic symmetric Jacobi max 100 sweeps, relative off-diagonal tolerance 1e-12, residual and orthogonality checks 1e-8; retained eigenvalues/PLS score variance rank threshold 1e-10 relative to leading/original scale. Explicit block if insufficient rank or nonfinite. Covariance rather than raw QR for spectral methods; potentially more sensitive to conditioning, guarded by rank checks. Deploy dense 1944-coefficient predictor, report latent rank separately (not effective degrees of freedom). Report shared Gram/eigen cost and per-configuration fit; PLS doesn't require shared G eigensystem. Tests rotated eigensystem, PCR vs supervised PLS on low-variance signal, fitted-response RRR rank, prediction restoration. No held-out data or new dependencies.
