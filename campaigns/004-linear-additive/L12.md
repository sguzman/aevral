# L12 bounded-grid MARS (pre-fit specification)
Six configurations (degree 1 or 2; max 16/32/64 non-intercept terms). Explicitly labeled mars-grid, not exhaustive Friedman MARS: resource-bounded forward search on 1024 evenly spaced TRAINING rows; candidate knots are sample quartiles, root plus at most four most recent eligible degree-one parents; distinct interaction variables. Rank pairs by approximate normalized residual cross-product improvement, then exact orthogonal least-squares gain for top eight pairs. Add reflected hinge pairs adaptively, orthogonalize twice, stop on no positive independent gain or cap, 900s guard. Fit all selected terms on ALL training rows by unregularized least squares; rank floor 1e-12 relative Gram diagonal. Backward deletion chooses exact RSS increase sum(beta_j²)/inverse Gram diagonal, refits each nested subset, selects minimum training GCV (effective count 1+2.5*nonintercept terms). No evaluation-guided pruning, no static GAM substitution. Record forward selection, deletion path, chosen terms, retained coefficients and actual runtime. Synthetic adaptive V-shape and exact deletion tests. No external dependencies. Up to five parallel MARS jobs while full polynomial job active. Approximation limitation: forward sample, restricted knot/parent/shortlist search; all training used for final coefficients and pruning.
