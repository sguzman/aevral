Nine configurations per manifest. Let z_t=y_t for d=D=0, z_t=y_t-y_(t-1) for d=1, or z_t=y_t-y_(t-24) for D=1. Model z_t=c+sum phi_j*z_(t-j)+theta*e_(t-1)+beta*x_(t-1)+e_t. Multiplicative seasonal AR(1) expands nonseasonal AR polynomial by (1-Phi*B^24), including negative cross-lags. AR(2) parameterized via partial autocorrelations k1,k2 (phi1=k1*(1-k2), phi2=k2), all partial autocorrelations, seasonal AR, MA bounded +/-0.98. Drift/intercept and exogenous coefficients bounded +/-4 normalized units. ARIMAX is explicitly conditional ARMAX on level or differenced temperature, with known calendar at preceding timestamp and weather fixed at forecast origin, including the conditional initialization history; it is not an exact full-series regression-with-ARIMA-errors estimator. Historical weather lags not used. 
For each of 2048 deterministic evenly spaced training origins, initialize innovations zero for unavailable lag warmup, filter over 72-hour history, evaluate only one-step innovation at t+1 (training label). Optimize mean squared final innovation by bounded deterministic coordinate pattern search: initial AR1=.5 others zero, initial step .25, <=96 sweeps, halve when improvement <=1e-9*max(previous objective,1e-6), accept finite improvements >1e-12, convergence step <=1e-4, report capped vs converged, 1000s fit guard. Finite capped fits are explicitly approximate, no claim exact ML or global optimum. Future innovations zero, integrate recursively. Fit and filter never access evaluation targets. Report coefficients, parameter count, sample size, optimization evaluations, objective, terminal step, convergence, time, RSS, lead/month scores.
