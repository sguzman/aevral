ETS additive innovations: forecast level + phi*trend + seasonal slot (when present). State update level += phi*trend + alpha*innovation; trend = phi*trend + beta*innovation (trend models); seasonal slot += gamma*innovation (AAA). ANN no trend/season; AAN undamped trend; AAdN damped trend; AAA additive trend plus seasonal period 24. Initialize level from mean first 24 history values; nonseasonal trend from difference of first/second 12-hour means divided by 12, seasonal trend zero and seasonal slots first day minus mean. Filter remaining 48 observed hours; future innovations zero. Fit grid on 2048 evenly spaced training origins by mean squared one-step final innovation: alpha .05,.2,.5,.8; beta 0,.02,.1,.2 if trend; gamma same grid as alpha if seasonal; phi .8,.9,.95,.98 for damped else 1. Enforce beta<=alpha, gamma<=1-alpha. Count fitted smoothing coefficients, not initialized per-window states. Conditional fixed-window ETS, not full-series maximum likelihood. Report all finite grid trials and selected coefficients.

Fit residual AR(1) or AR(6) plus intercept on all training rows using last observed residuals and next-hour residual target. Two-stage OLS, not jointly optimized GLS/ML. Predict harmonic at known future timestamp and recursively extrapolate residual. QR rank tolerance 1e-10; nonfinite predictions fail common scorer; no clipping.
