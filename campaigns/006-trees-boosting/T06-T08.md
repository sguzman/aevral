Two configurations each: xgboost-64/128, lightgbm-64/128, catboost-64/128. Per-lead scalar models, all 80 normalized inputs and normalized targets, same 24576 training rows, full evaluation. LightGBM official CLI GBDT regression, num_leaves16, max_depth4, learning_rate0.1, max_bin64, min_data_in_leaf32, 2 threads, deterministic=true, force_col_wise=true, seed. CatBoost CPU RMSE symmetric trees, depth4, learning_rate0.1, 2 threads, seed; numeric features, no eval set, no best-model selection, pinned remaining defaults (including stochastic split regularization/bootstrap). XGBoost and LightGBM use deterministic configurations (one annual seed42); CatBoost stochastic (three annual seeds, 42/123/2026).

Fit/score separately; upstream fit elapsed includes batch prediction and serialization overhead, not directly comparable to pure training time. 1200s elapsed guard including first-lead preflight extrapolation (2x headroom); CLI subprocess timeout, native XGB guard between rounds. No eval early stopping. No paid compute or local downloads. Synthetic smoke test first (known two-cluster regression, label-free prediction, actual model counts); only submit weather CI after all three succeed.
